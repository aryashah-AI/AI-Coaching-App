# Triple Jump AI Coach — Project Overview

This guide explains, in simple terms, what the Triple Jump AI Coach does, how
to use the dashboard, and what insights you will receive (annotated video,
charts, and a performance report).

---

## Domain background: Triple Jump phases

- **Approach**: run-up phase before the first takeoff (not scored as a phase
  but shown on the timeline).
- **Hop**: takeoff from one foot, landing on the same foot.
- **Step**: takeoff from the same foot, landing on the other foot.
- **Jump**: final takeoff from the other foot, landing in the sand pit.

Key biomechanical signals across phases include lower-limb joint angles (hip,
knee, ankle), airborne vs. ground-contact states, and smooth phase
transitions (hop → step → jump).

---

## How it works (at a glance)

1. You upload a triple jump video in the web dashboard.
2. The app tracks your movement and estimates key body angles over time.
3. It detects when you take off and land, and splits the motion into phases:
   hop, step, jump.
4. It summarizes your technique per phase and compares it to strong examples.
5. You receive an overall score, phase-by-phase highlights, and targeted
   coaching tips.

---

## Using the dashboard

- Training Mode (for coaches and benchmark clips)
    - Upload two or more strong example videos.
    - The app learns typical joint angles for hop, step, and jump.
    - You’ll get a simple confirmation that a training profile was saved.
- Analysis Mode (for athletes)
    - Upload your jump video.
    - The app analyzes your technique, compares it to the trained profile, and
      shows results instantly on the results page.
    - Works best with a clear side view, stable camera, and one athlete in
      frame.

---

## What you’ll see

- Annotated video: your original clip with clean overlays showing phases,
  events, and live angle readouts.
- Clear score: an overall 0–100 score that reflects how closely your technique
  matches strong examples.
- Phase-by-phase breakdown: strengths and gaps for hop, step, and jump.
- Coaching tips: 2–3 focused suggestions to improve your next attempt.
- Quick charts: simple visuals that summarize movement and angles over time.

---

## Exports you can download

- Annotated video of your jump
- A concise performance report (overall score, phase notes, recommendations)
- A summary image with simple charts

---

## Best results: capture checklist

- Use a clear side view with good lighting.
- Keep the camera steady (tripod if possible).
- Make sure the athlete is fully visible throughout the approach, hop, step,
  and jump.

---

## Notes and considerations

- Results depend on video quality and viewing angle.
- Moving or shaky cameras can reduce accuracy.
- Training quality matters: better benchmark clips lead to better guidance.

---

## About the AI

The system uses modern computer vision to follow movement and estimate joint
angles. It converts those measurements into simple, practical insights for
athletes and coaches.
